## HEALTH RATING SYSTEM

**Your Goals:**
- Cardiovascular health (familial hypercholesterolemia management)
- Lean muscle gain (protein-focused)
- Calorie control (energy balance for training)
- Smoking reduction support

**Rating Criteria:**
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **OPTIMAL:** High protein, reasonable calories, lean preparation, supports all goals
- ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **EXCELLENT:** High protein, supports muscle/health goals, minor area for optimization
- ⭐⭐⭐ **GOOD:** Balanced nutrition, decent protein, suitable for regular eating
- ⭐⭐ **MODERATE:** Higher calories or fat, use strategically
- ⭐ **OCCASIONAL:** High in saturated fat or calories, limit frequency

## CUSTOM MEAL ENTRY GUIDE

**When a meal isn't listed, estimate using:**

1. **Protein Source** (grams, size)
   - Chicken breast: 35g protein per 3.5 oz (100g)
   - Beef steak: 26g protein per 3.5 oz
   - Fish: 25g protein per 3.5 oz
   - Pork: 27g protein per 3.5 oz
   - Eggs: 6g protein per egg

2. **Carb Source** (estimated from typical portions)
   - Rice: 45g carbs per cooked cup
   - Pasta: 40g carbs per cooked cup
   - Potato: 37g carbs per medium baked
   - Bread: 15-20g carbs per slice
   - Vegetables: 5-10g carbs per cup (cooked)

3. **Fat Source**
   - Oil/butter: 14g fat per tablespoon
   - Cheese: 7-9g fat per ounce
   - Nuts: 14g fat per ounce
   - Avocado: 11g fat per half

4. **Calorie Calculation**
   - Protein: 4 calories per gram
   - Carbs: 4 calories per gram
   - Fat: 9 calories per gram

**Example Custom Meal:**
- Grilled chicken breast (4 oz): 140 cal, 35g protein
- White rice (1 cup cooked): 200 cal, 45g carbs
- Olive oil (1 tsp): 40 cal, 4.5g fat
- Broccoli (2 cups): 70 cal, 14g carbs
- **Total: 450 cal, 35g protein, 59g carbs, 4.5g fat**
